Tree of light

Somewhere deep in the forest of Winland, a tree grows that carries more than fruit — it carries hope.

Jump into Tree of Light - it's this fantasy adventure where honestly, every little thing you do actually counts for something. As you wander through the forest, old symbols start lighting up, your buddies get tougher, and the whole world kinda... notices what you're doing.
