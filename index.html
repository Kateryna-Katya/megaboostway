The Forest That Remembers

Winland isn't just some pretty backdrop - this place is actually alive. The trees literally talk to you, there are symbols everywhere if you know where to look, and man, the deeper you explore, the more crazy stuff you learn about this place.

A Path Toward the Tree of Light

Lupine and his buddy Mino? They're not in it for the power trip. They are answering a call that leads them toward the legendary Tree of Light, said to grow the most radiant fruits in the forest.

Scrolls of the Old Forest

Hidden scrolls display ancient patterns passed down through generations. When activated, they provide additional strength and gently influence the flow of the journey.
It doesn't feel like the game's stopping to show you stuff - more like you're actually finding cool things.
